## Registration scope

This OSF registration is **retrospective**. The principal software development, simulations, statistical analyses and confirmatory experiments were completed before the registration was created. The registration therefore must not be interpreted as a preregistration.

Its purpose is to preserve a timestamped methodological and provenance record connecting:

1. the research question and study design;
2. the computational model and synthetic disruption assumptions;
3. the paired Monte Carlo and coupled-world experimental design;
4. the confirmatory 80/80 reliability-boundary estimand;
5. the statistical inference and bootstrap uncertainty procedure;
6. the frozen GitHub/Zenodo research-software release; and
7. subsequent scholarly outputs derived from the completed investigation.

## Scientific boundary

The project is a computational research prototype and reproducibility package. The synthetic `severity` parameter is not earthquake magnitude, PGA, an engineering fragility measure, an official damage estimate, or an operational emergency threshold. The v1.0 study is a Beykoz district-scale pilot and is not a validated operational estimate for İstanbul or Türkiye.
