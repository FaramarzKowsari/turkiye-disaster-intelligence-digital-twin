## What is frozen

- public AFAD/OSM data-access layer
- synthetic district-scale scenario engine
- Greedy and Global Minimum-Cost assignment baselines
- paired Monte Carlo inference
- coupled severity-world experiment design
- resource-frontier and phase-transition study
- confirmatory 80/80 reliability-boundary analysis
- bootstrap uncertainty intervals
- GitHub Actions research workflows
- v0.8 scientific-results evidence package
- trilingual research website and README

## Final empirical statement

For the Beykoz pilot and the specified synthetic disruption model, the Global Minimum-Cost
Assignment 80/80 boundary rises from 0.1425 at 12 responders to 0.1625 at 32 responders.
Additional responders extend the reliability region, with diminishing marginal gains.

Global Minimum-Cost Assignment also reduces total response-time cost relative to sequential Greedy
across every tested cell in the two confirmatory grids after Holm correction.

## What v1.0 does not claim

This release does not claim earthquake prediction, certified structural damage modelling,
casualty estimation, official emergency status, or an operational dispatch capability.

## Next work requires a new research question

Possible future directions include authoritative infrastructure layers, calibrated fragility
models, hospital and shelter capacity, multi-hazard coupling, learned policies, real-event
retrospective validation and cross-district transfer tests. They are intentionally outside v1.0.
