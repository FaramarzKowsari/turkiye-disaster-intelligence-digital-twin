## Scope

This preprint studies emergency-response accessibility under synthetic post-earthquake road disruption using a reproducible graph-based Monte Carlo framework for Beykoz, Istanbul. It evaluates service-reliability boundaries across responder levels and compares a sequential Greedy assignment strategy with a Global Minimum-Cost assignment strategy.

## Important interpretation boundary

The disruption-severity parameter is a synthetic network-stress variable. It is not earthquake magnitude, peak ground acceleration (PGA), a physical damage measure, an engineering fragility threshold, or an operational safety threshold.
