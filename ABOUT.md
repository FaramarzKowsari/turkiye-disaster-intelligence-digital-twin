# About the Project

## Türkiye Disaster Intelligence Digital Twin

**Türkiye Disaster Intelligence Digital Twin** is a reproducible graph-based research platform for studying how emergency-response accessibility changes when a road network is progressively disrupted after an earthquake-like stress scenario. The v1.0 research cycle focuses on the Beykoz district of İstanbul as a controlled pilot environment.

The platform combines public AFAD catalogue access, OpenStreetMap road and emergency-facility data, reproducible synthetic road failures, paired Monte Carlo experiments, graph-based responder assignment, phase-transition analysis and world-cluster bootstrap inference. Its central scientific question is not whether an earthquake can be predicted, but where emergency response shifts from a resource-allocation problem to a network-connectivity problem.

### Current scientific release

The frozen v1.0.0 evidence estimates an 80/80 reliability boundary: the synthetic disruption level at which the probability of preserving at least 80% incident reachability crosses 80%. Under the tested model, the Global Minimum-Cost boundary increases from **0.1425 with 12 responders** to **0.1625 with 32 responders**, while the marginal gain from additional responders diminishes.

The severity variable is a **synthetic network-disruption control parameter**. It is not earthquake magnitude, PGA, an engineering fragility measure or an official emergency threshold.

# Sobre el proyecto

**Türkiye Disaster Intelligence Digital Twin** es una plataforma de investigación reproducible basada en grafos para estudiar cómo cambia la accesibilidad de la respuesta de emergencia cuando una red viaria sufre interrupciones progresivas. El ciclo v1.0 utiliza Beykoz, Estambul, como entorno piloto controlado.

La plataforma integra datos públicos de AFAD y OpenStreetMap, fallos viarios sintéticos reproducibles, experimentos Monte Carlo emparejados, asignación de recursos basada en grafos, análisis de transición de fase e inferencia bootstrap por mundo.

- **DOI de v1.0.0:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21903851
- **Concept DOI para todas las versiones:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21903850

La variable `severity` es un parámetro sintético de interrupción de red; no representa magnitud sísmica ni un umbral operativo oficial.
