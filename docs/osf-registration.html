WHAT THIS REGISTRATION MEANS

A timestamped methodological and provenance layer

The registration freezes the research design, computational workflow, estimands, inferential procedures, reproducibility architecture, limitations, and evidence chain associated with the completed Türkiye Disaster Intelligence Digital Twin v1.0.0 research cycle.

It connects the research question to the frozen software release and provides a persistent record that can be cited independently from the software DOI.

Retrospective, not preregistered

The principal software development, simulations, statistical analyses and confirmatory experiments were completed before this OSF registration was created. It must therefore not be represented as a preregistration.

Scientific boundary
