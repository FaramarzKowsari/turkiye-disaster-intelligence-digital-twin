RESEARCH QUESTION

Can graph-aware allocation improve post-earthquake emergency response?

The project compares transparent baselines, constrained optimisation and later learned policies under identical stochastic disruption scenarios.

01

Data & provenance

AFAD events, OpenStreetMap networks and public datasets enter versioned, auditable pipelines.

02

Scenario engine

Controlled road disruption and emergency-demand simulations preserve seeds and uncertainty.

03

Decision intelligence

Greedy dispatch, graph optimisation and future MARL are evaluated with the same response and fairness metrics.
